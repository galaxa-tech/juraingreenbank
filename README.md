# Jurain Green Bank (জুরাইন গ্রিন ব্যাংক)

> **টেকসই কৃষি, নিরাপদ খাদ্য, সবুজ সমৃদ্ধি**  
> *Sustainable Agriculture, Safe Food Sovereignty, and Living Soil Conservation.*  
> An initiative by **Jurain Sabuj Joddha (জুরাইন সবুজ যোদ্ধা)**, East Jurain, Dhaka, Bangladesh.

---

## 🌟 Pro-Bono Digital Partnership
This platform was proudly designed, developed, and voluntarily contributed by **[Astramonks](https://astramonks.space)** to champion grassroots environmental resilience and climate sovereignty in urban Dhaka.

---

## 🌿 Core Features
- **Bilingual Interface**: Seamless instant toggling between Bengali (বাংলা) and English with localStorage persistence.
- **Astramonks Signature Aesthetic**: Elevated typography, organic forest & emerald palette, frosted glassmorphism, animated leaf/seed canvas particles, and signature micro-animated CTA pill buttons.
- **Complete Multi-Page Architecture**:
  - `index.html`: Hero with dynamic canvas particles, why Jurain needs a green bank, 5 core pillars, live interactive bento gallery, chemical urea vs. living soil comparison, household eco-impact calculator, testimonials, and FAQs.
  - `about.html`: In-depth history of Jurain Sabuj Joddha, East Jurain's environmental reality, guiding ethics, and the Astramonks pro-bono stewardship.
  - `programs.html`: Deep operational guides for all 5 core initiatives (Reused Cup Nursery, Night Potting Drives, Indigenous Seed Bank, Biochar Workshops, Rooftop Greening).
  - `gallery.html`: Filterable Bento media archive with full high-resolution lightbox modal.
  - `request.html`: Interactive free sapling & organic compost request wizard with unique reference code generation (`JGB-2026-XXXX`).
  - `volunteer.html`: Volunteer squad registration and community pledge hub.
  - `contact.html`: Physical landmark directions from Jurain Railgate, Postogola Bridge, and Jatrabari; direct call, email, and social links.
- **Full Backend Readiness (Supabase)**:
  - PostgreSQL schema provided in `supabase/schema.sql` for `volunteers`, `sapling_requests`, and `contact_messages`.
  - Resilient client (`assets/js/supabase.js`) that automatically syncs with Supabase when keys are provided, with offline fallback.
- **Primary SEO & Metadata**:
  - OpenGraph & Twitter cards.
  - Schema.org JSON-LD NGO structure.
  - `sitemap.xml` and `robots.txt`.
